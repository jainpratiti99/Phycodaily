Why now

The gap isn't new. The moment to close it is.

Three shifts are converging at the same time, and none of them existed at this scale five years ago.

Diets have industrialised faster than health has kept up

Ultra-processed foods now dominate family breakfast tables, and diet-linked conditions like diabetes are climbing in exactly the households we sell to — creating demand for alternatives, not another lecture.

Supplement fatigue is real and measurable

Consumers have cycled through enough fortified powders and "magic scoops" to be skeptical of them by default. Trust has shifted toward whole, recognisable ingredients — which is where Pantry already lives.

The ingredient science is finally supply-ready

Microalgae and functional-ingredient supply chains have matured enough in the last few years to move from lab curiosity to café and B2B customer — which is what makes Labs viable today, not in a decade.

Built by a scientist. Designed for families.
