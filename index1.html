The Science of Millets

Why ancient grains for modern families

🌾

Foxtail Millet (Setaria italica)

Low glycaemic index. Rich in complex carbohydrates, dietary fibre and minerals including iron and calcium.

🌿

Pearl Millet (Pennisetum glaucum)

Highest iron content among common cereals. Rich in zinc and B vitamins. Naturally gluten-free.

🌱

Finger Millet (Eleusine coracana)

Exceptionally high calcium — relevant for children and lactating mothers. FAO-recognised as climate-resilient and nutritionally superior.

🌾

Barnyard Millet (Echinochloa frumentacea)

Highest fibre content among millets. Very low glycaemic index. Fast prep for everyday family cooking.

🌾

Sorghum / Jowar (Sorghum bicolor)

Rich in antioxidant phenolic compounds and iron. Naturally gluten-free. Fermentation improves mineral bioavailability.

🌿

Little Millet (Panicum sumatrense)

High in dietary fibre and essential fatty acids. Comparable protein quality to rice and wheat.
